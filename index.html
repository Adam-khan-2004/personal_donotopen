There are things I don't say enough

What I see
when I think of you.

“

I still remember the day I first saw you. I am sorry I forgot what you wore that day: a pink hijab with a white kurta with pink flowers on it and a brown bag. I am so thankful to Allah that you came to that gym event that day and that I got to see you.

When I saw you for the first time, I thought you were an outsider and that I would never see you again. In my heart, I had already rejected myself because you were, and you still are, so pretty that I couldn't even imagine being able to say hi to you. You were always out of my league. I looked at you and got frozen for some time because of your beauty.

But that was a crush. I think real love comes when you get to know the person and then fall in love with that person more and more every day, just like I fell for you. I am still shocked at how I followed you, how I said hi, and how we are still talking. It feels like a beautiful dream, and I don't want to wake up from this dream ever.

I feel your absence in my life every day. I miss our sessions. I miss when I used to sit with you and admire you like I had never seen someone like you before. I miss dropping you at the barrier. I miss opening the door for you. I miss carrying an umbrella for you. I miss getting flowers for you. I miss all your jokes. I miss your beautiful talks about life, deen, and philosophy. I miss everything about you.

Some of my most beautiful memories were when I drank from the same straw that you drank from, the chocolate paratha and chai, and when you offered me water from your glass and then drank from the same glass yourself. You said you had never drunk from someone else's glass before, but you left that thing for me. It was your love language, and it still makes me emotional.

When you gave me a chance for my proposal, I was so happy that I wanted to dance and run around the entire campus. I felt so good knowing that you trusted me, and I promise I will never break that trust. It is so precious to me.

The umbrella moment... I was so happy that day. I can't even explain it. And opening the door for you, every little moment, everything meant so much to me.

After knowing you in person, I am more in love with you than ever. The vibe matches so well between us. You have made me so calm and happy, and I feel so much peace and completeness with you. It feels like I found everything I ever wanted, like you make me complete.

If I have to say something about you, I will say this:

You are like a beautiful dream to me that never makes me want to wake up, and I want to live in that dream forever. I want to see us grow together, get old together, and never get tired of each other. I want to love you like nobody has ever loved you before.

I was a boy, but you made me a man. And there is still a lot of room for betterment. I promise I will make myself better every day.
